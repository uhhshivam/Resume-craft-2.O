About ResumeCraft

ResumeCraft is a free resume creation tool designed to help students, freshers and experienced professionals create clear, professional resumes. Build your resume, customize its design and download a copy for your career journey.

Privacy Policy

Your resume information is processed in your browser. Drafts can be saved in your browser's local storage. Information saved in your browser is not automatically sent to ResumeCraft. Avoid using public or shared devices for private resume information, and clear saved drafts when finished.

Third-party services, such as the PDF generation library, may load from their own providers.
